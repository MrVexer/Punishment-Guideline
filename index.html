Staff Punishment Guidelines

Staff Guidance

These punishment levels are guidelines and do not guarantee a fixed outcome. Staff should consider severity, evidence, intent, prior history, cooperation, impact on others, and any aggravating or mitigating circumstances.

Punishment stages may be skipped where conduct is severe, deliberate, repeated, malicious, or presents a risk to the server or community.
